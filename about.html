About School.Media

We're building the media network for schools — where families find the right school, students tell their stories, and schools reach the people looking for them.

Our mission

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes, yet good information is scattered, biased or hidden behind sales calls. School.Media brings it together: honest guides, side-by-side comparisons, real reviews, scholarships and events — free for families.

We also believe students are the best storytellers about their schools. Through contests, our student reporter program and the video hub, we give young creators a real audience and real rewards.

What we stand for

  • Clarity — sponsored placements are always labelled
  • Privacy — families control who contacts them
  • Opportunity — prizes, scholarships and paid work for students
  • Independence — ratings are never for sale
