Frequently asked questions

Is School.Media free for families?

Yes. Searching, comparing, School Match, scholarships and contests are free. We're funded by advertising, school partnerships and donations.

How are featured schools chosen?

Featured placements are paid partnerships and are always labelled. Rankings and ratings are not for sale.

Who sees my information when I request school info?

Only the school(s) you choose or are matched with, and only for admissions purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

My school isn't listed. How do we get added?

Schools can claim or create a free profile in minutes.
