Donation FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?

School.Media is an independent media company, so donations are not tax-deductible unless stated on your receipt. They directly fund our free programs.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, anytime from your receipt email or by contacting us.

Can I donate in another currency?

Our payment partner accepts most major cards and currencies.
