Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how School.Media ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use school.media. Please have your legal counsel review and adapt this template to your jurisdiction before launch.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, email, phone, location, student grade, preferences and messages you submit through forms (school inquiries, School Match, contests, scholarships, jobs, donations, newsletters).
  • Automatic information: device, browser, pages viewed, referral and campaign (UTM) parameters, collected via cookies and similar technologies.

How we use information

  • To deliver the services you request (matches, school info requests, event RSVPs, contest entries, job applications).
  • To share your inquiry with the school(s) you choose or are matched with — only with your consent.
  • To send newsletters and alerts you opted into (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To measure and improve the site, and to show advertising.

Advertising & cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. We ask for consent before enabling non-essential cookies where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland). You can change your choice via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Children's privacy

Our family services are intended for parents and guardians. Students under 13 (or the age required in their country) must not submit personal information without verifiable parental consent. Contest and reporter applications from minors require parent/guardian consent. Contact us to remove a child's information.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it with schools you choose, service providers (hosting, email, forms, payments, analytics) under contract, and where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, India DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and withdraw consent. Email privacy@school.media.

Retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above and protect it with reasonable safeguards.
